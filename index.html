How to read this explorer

Use the year and season controls to change the map and charts. Filter by division or select a district to focus the trend chart. The values are generated for interface testing only; they do not represent observed land-surface temperature.

For a scientific version, replace the demonstration data generator with quality-screened satellite land-surface temperature summaries and document the sensor, acquisition period, cloud masking, units, and district aggregation method.

Land-surface temperature is the temperature of the ground or surface observed by a sensor. It is not the same as near-surface air temperature reported in weather forecasts.
