Controls & interpretation

Change the year, season, palette, and intensity to explore how an interactive thermal dashboard can work. The values are generated algorithmically for this demo; they are not evidence of actual district heat conditions.

The canvas shows a stylized Bangladesh-inspired silhouette and thermal field, not a precise geographic boundary. No external map tiles, scripts, fonts, or boundary data are required.
